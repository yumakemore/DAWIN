1. Core Model, Kernel & Dataset Modules (5 Files)

• dawin_core.py: Implements the drop-in DAWINLinear primitive with paired-real quadrature propagation, parallel associative prefix scan, exact serial fallback, and component ablation toggles.
• dawin_triton.py: Implements the multi-block chunked Triton parallel associative scan kernel and custom autograd bridge for GPU acceleration across arbitrarily long sequences.
• dawin_models.py: Provides the three continuous-time DAWIN backbones (DAWINMLP, DAWINTransformer, DAWINGNN), five parameter-matched comparators (MatchedReLUMLP, MatchedTimeTransformer, MatchedNeuralCDE, MatchedS5, MatchedMamba, plus MatchedGRUGNN), and the parameter-matching width solver.
• dawin_datasets.py: Provides data loaders, normalization hygiene, and metric helpers for MuJoCo irregular dynamics, mask-aware PhysioNet 2012, and ETH/UCY + JAAD trajectories.
• dawin_diagnostics.py: Implements the RQ3 Δ t-perturbation suite (batch-shuffled and constant-mean intervals), RQ4 GPU throughput/memory profiling, and paired bootstrap / Holm-Bonferroni statistical tests.

• export_dawin_latex_tables.py: Compiles master_benchmark_summary_<precision>.json and rq4_b200_scaling_<precision>.json into NeurIPS, ICLR, or ICML booktabs \begin{table*} blocks.
• plot_dawin_figures.py: Renders publication-ready .pdf and .png figures for RQ1/RQ2 accuracy comparisons, RQ3 causal timing interventions, and RQ4 NVIDIA B200 scaling curves.
